1.The service

Tansi gives shops a live queue, a point of sale, kitchen and TV screens, customer records and rewards, stock and books, and chats on LINE, Messenger and Telegram; it gives their guests a way to join a queue, follow their ticket, order at a table and collect rewards. We may add, change or remove features as Tansi grows.

We may introduce paid plans. We will tell shops before anything is charged, and nothing is charged without the shop agreeing to it.

  • You serve your guests: what happens at your shop — the wait, the food, the service — is between you and them.

4.For guests
