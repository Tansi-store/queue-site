Questions owners ask first.

Anything else, ask us. We'd rather answer than have you guess.

Do guests need to install an app?

No. They scan your QR or use your shop's LINE, Messenger or Telegram, and the ticket opens in their phone's browser.

How do guests know it's their turn?

Their ticket page turns green, chimes and vibrates; a LINE or Telegram message arrives (Messenger inside its 24-hour window); and your TV shows the number and announces it.

What if a guest doesn't come back?

After the minutes you choose, the ticket is skipped on its own. If they turn up later, staff put them back at the front in one tap.

Do I need special hardware?

No. Any phone, tablet or computer with a browser runs it, and any TV with a browser shows the waiting list. A camera or a USB scanner for check-in is optional: staff can tap the guest's number instead.

Is it only for restaurants?

No. Barbers, salons, spas, clinics, KTV, rooms let by the hour, shops and marts use it too: guests can pick their stylist or doctor, rooms can be charged by the hour, by the visit or by the person, and a mart scans barcodes at the till.

Which payments can I take?

Cash with change, PromptPay QR, cards, and wallet apps such as KBZPay, WavePay, AYA Pay, CB Pay, TrueMoney and MMQR. For a wallet payment a paired phone photographs the slip, so the bill keeps its proof.

Can I run more than one branch?

Yes. One shop holds every branch: a shared menu with branch prices, staff who work at one branch or all of them, and sales for each branch and the whole shop.

Can my accountant work from it?

Yes. Each day closes with a Z report, every figure opens the bills behind it, a refund is booked on the day it's made so a closed day never changes, card and wallet takings are matched against the bank, and the books download as an Excel workbook or a journal file for Xero or QuickBooks.

Does it keep track of stock?

If you switch it on. Stock is kept per branch and moves when a bill is paid; dishes made of ingredients use them; purchase orders, transfers between branches, counts and adjustments keep it true; and you see its value and your gross profit. A shop or mart counts stock from the start.

Can ChatGPT or Claude work in my shop?

Yes, as you and only as far as you allow: connect your own AI app, tick which of your permissions it gets, and it asks before it changes anything. Everything it does is in the activity log, and you can disconnect it any time.

Who can see my shop's data?

Only your shop. Every shop's data is kept apart, chat app credentials are encrypted, staff see only what their role allows, and voids, refunds and other sensitive actions are recorded with who did them and who approved them.
